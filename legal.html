Returns, refunds & replacements

Decals are custom-made to the artwork, size, material, and specifications selected at checkout. Custom products are not returnable for change of mind, buyer’s remorse, or an incorrect size selected by the customer.

If an item arrives damaged, defective, or materially different from the confirmed order, contact us within 7 calendar days of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We may provide a replacement or refund when appropriate.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Allow 5–10 business days for the payment provider or bank to post the refund. Original shipping charges are refundable only when the problem is our error or the item was defective.

Cancellations & changes

Request a cancellation or correction as soon as possible by email. We can generally cancel or modify an order before production begins. Once printing, cutting, or weeding has started, the order may not be cancellable or changeable.

Submitting an order does not guarantee cancellation; production may begin promptly after payment.
