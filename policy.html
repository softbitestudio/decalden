Returns and replacements

Every decal is custom-made from the artwork, size, and specifications you submit. Because of that, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for buyer’s remorse, incorrect sizing selected by the customer, or a change of mind after production begins.

Damaged or defective items: If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or defective, we’ll work with you on a free replacement. Please email us within 7 calendar days of delivery with your order number and clear photos showing the item as it arrived.

Refunds and payment disputes

Refunds are reviewed case by case. A full refund may be available for an order cancelled before production begins, or for a verified damaged or misprinted item when a replacement cannot reasonably be provided.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Please allow 5–10 business days for your financial institution to post the refund.

If something went wrong with a charge or order, please contact us first at bunrec@softbitestudio.com so we have an opportunity to make it right.

Order cancellations

You may request a full cancellation within approximately 2 hours of placing your order, provided production has not begun. Custom printing, cutting, and weeding may begin quickly; once an order enters production, it cannot be cancelled or modified.

To request a cancellation, email us as soon as possible with your order number.

  • We may refuse or cancel orders containing unauthorized copyrighted or trademarked material, unlawful content, or material that violates our safety standards.
  • We ship physical goods domestically and internationally where available.
  • International customers are responsible for applicable customs fees, import duties, and destination-country taxes.
  • Shipping availability and delivery estimates may vary by destination and carrier.

Promotions and discounts

  • Promotional and discount codes are valid only during the stated promotional period and under the terms shown with the offer.
  • Discounts cannot be applied retroactively, combined with other offers, or exchanged for cash unless explicitly stated.
  • Soft Bite Studio may modify, pause, or end a promotion when necessary. Any offer-specific terms shown at checkout take precedence.
